### Kipato
**Offline-first income record for informal workers**

Log daily cash earnings with no signal, import M-PESA history, and generate a verifiable income statement a SACCO or lender can open without an account.

`React 19` `Flask` `PostgreSQL` `IndexedDB`

**[▸ Live](https://kipato.vercel.app)** · [Source](https://github.com/gnm7208/kipato)

### Hesabu
**Chama reconciliation, close-the-books tooling**

Parses a savings group's M-PESA messages into a dispute-proof ledger — contributions, arrears, monthly statements — so the treasurer stops matching SMS to names by hand.

`React` `TypeScript` `Flask` `PostgreSQL`

**[▸ Live](https://hesabu-sigma.vercel.app)** · [Source](https://github.com/gnm7208/Hesabu)

### Fundi Connect
**Verified services marketplace with M-PESA escrow**

Connects households with verified craftspeople. The customer's money is locked with the platform, not handed to the fundi. Identity verification, geo-search, ratings, Daraja.

`React 19` `TypeScript` `Flask 3` `SQLAlchemy 2`

**[▸ Live](https://fundi-connect-pi.vercel.app)** · [Source](https://github.com/gnm7208/fundi-connect)
